# Changelog

Every commit adds an entry here, newest first.

## 1.0.5 — November 25, 2026

- **Keep for (days)** on each **Note to yourself**: choose how many days the note keeps showing in Last time. The default is 1 day (24 hours), counted from the first time you see the note in a workout, not from when you wrote it. After that it's hidden, but it stays in that day's workout in History. Notes you already wrote get 1 day, starting the next time you see them.
- **Skip rest** now asks "Are you sure? It's recommended to rest 2 to 3 minutes between sets." Turn it off in Settings with **Ask before skipping rest**.

## 1.0.4 — November 16, 2026

- **Bug fix:** exercise videos now show the whole clip in the workout and the workout editor, instead of a zoomed-in strip that cut off the top and bottom. Tall videos get plain bars on the sides.
- **Bug fix:** when you skip a workout and type a reason, the box moves up above the keyboard instead of hiding behind it.
- **Bug fix:** the Note to yourself and Add exercise boxes move up above the keyboard during a workout.

## 1.0.3 — November 5, 2026

- **Last time** is now a tidy panel instead of one long line: the date at the top, then one row per group of sets with the count in its own column, like "3 sets: 10 reps × 40 lbs". Cardio shows one row per entry.
- **Note to yourself** for each exercise: during a workout, tap **Note to yourself** (next to Add exercise) to write a reminder like "Go up to 45 lbs". Next time you do that exercise, it shows in its **Last time** panel. You can also edit it when you review or edit the workout.
- **Fill in set** in the Last time panel copies that set's reps and weight from last time into your current set, so you don't have to type them. Tap it again on each new set; you can still change the numbers before logging.

## 1.0.2 — October 30, 2026

- **Last time** for every exercise: during a workout, each exercise shows what you logged for it the last time you did it, with the number of sets, like "Last time (Fri, Oct 30): 10 reps × 40 lbs (3 sets)". Only days you did at least one set of that exercise count, so an exercise you skipped keeps showing the session before. Cardio shows its time. Use it to see where you're improving and what to focus on.
- **View workout** now shows your notes under a **Notes** heading, only when you wrote some.
- New **app icon**: the Ironweek plate, including Android's themed (monochrome) icons.
- New **splash screen** with the IRONWEEK logo while the app opens, in a light and a dark version that follow your phone's theme.

## 1.0.1 — October 28, 2026

- The rest timer alarm now plays as a real alarm, so you hear it even when the phone is on vibrate or locked.
- New **Rest alarm sound** setting: pick Classic alarm, Digital watch, Rising siren or Ringing bell. Picking one plays a preview.
- The evening-before reminder now reads like a calendar event ("Upper Body", "Tomorrow at 8:00 AM").
- New **Evening-before sound** setting: pick Calendar chime, Soft marimba or Phone default.
- Two workouts can no longer have the same name (capitals and extra spaces don't count as different).
- On Android, the screen now moves up when the keyboard opens, so the field you're typing in stays above the keyboard instead of hiding behind it (workout editor and workout logging).
- The keyboard stays open after you press ✓, so you can type several exercises in a row. Scroll the list or press Back to hide it.
- **Cardio inside workouts:** when editing a workout (like Upper Body), tap **Add cardio** to add a timed item. It starts as **Treadmill**; its dropdown switches it to Stationary bike, Elliptical, Rowing machine, Stair climber, Outdoor run, Walk, or **Other** to type your own. During the workout it has a clock that counts up: **Start**, **Pause**/**Resume**, then **Finish** to add your average heart rate and calories burned (both optional). It's saved like "12 min · 142 bpm · 310 kcal". Your existing workouts and history are not changed.
- The ✓ next to Reps and Weight (log a set without starting the rest timer) is now hidden by default. Turn it back on in Settings with **Log without resting button**.
- New **About** section in Settings shows the app version, and **What's new** opens this changelog inside the app.
